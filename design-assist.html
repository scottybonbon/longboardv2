Four steps, in order

01 Technical Design Review The vision is reviewed against extrusion, coating, and installation before anything is promised. Free, and occasionally the answer is no — which is the point of asking early. Includes material estimates and detailed shop drawings.
02 Conceptual proposal What the extrusion can be, drawn: profile direction, finish, system, and the constraints that survived the review. A mock-up fee applies where physical mock-ups are built.
03 Contract Scope, schedule, and numbers in writing. The romance stays in the extrusion.
04 Build Custom extrusions, unique powder-coat finishes, CNC fabrication, prefabrication — designed, extruded, finished, and shipped from one building in Abbotsford.
