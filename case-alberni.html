Kengo Kuma’s first Canadian tower asked for wood where wood couldn’t go: curved, high, and exposed for decades.

02

03

The answer was 26,000+ linear feet of custom Longboard extrusion in National Walnut — designed, extruded, and finished in Abbotsford.

04
