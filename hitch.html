Technical specifications

A façade anchor is judged on three counts: how much heat it lets out, how many holes it makes, and how long it holds. HITCH™ answers with numbers — up to 94% thermal efficiency at standard 32" × 48" spacing, effective R-values above R60, up to 50% fewer clips than conventional continuous-girt systems, and cladding loads to 15 psf under high wind and seismic conditions.

It is certified as an Advanced Component by the Passive House Institute, carries a 50-year design service life and a 15-year system warranty, and anchors to masonry, concrete, wood, and steel. When the building outlives its first face, boards unclip: recladding without demolition.

Fewer clips is not just thermal arithmetic — it is schedule arithmetic. Up to 50% fewer anchors means up to half the layout, drilling, and flashing time at the substrate, on any of the four supported structures. Insulation from 1" to 16" installs continuously, without cutting around girts.

The system was designed by the people who extrude the boards it carries, so board, rail, and clip arrive as one system from one building in Abbotsford. And the install works in reverse: boards unclip for service access or full recladding.
