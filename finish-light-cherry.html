The chemistry

Light Cherry is not a photograph of cherry and it is not a laminate. The board is extruded, powder-coated, and cured; a film carrying the grain image is then drawn against the coat under heat and vacuum until the inks pass into it as gas and settle inside. The image is in the finish, not on it — there is no wrap to peel, no veneer to silver, nothing between the color and the aluminum but chemistry.

Process constants — temperature, dwell, registration — are held in Abbotsford. The catalogue does not publish them; neither does this page.
