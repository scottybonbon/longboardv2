Systems — the signature mechanism

Link & Lock™

Two pieces. The first one holds the building; the second one hides the evidence.

The mechanism

Fig. 1 — Link & Lock™ two-piece batten, section · open and locked Schematic — not to scale
THE SEAM IS THE SYSTEM. The base carries the load; the cap locks over it and closes the joint. What the wall shows is the reveal — the same 1px rule this site is drawn with. NOTHING TO SEE. Fastening is hidden: every screw disappears behind the cap, and matching end caps close the exposed ends of every run.

Orientations

The same two pieces run vertical, horizontal, or free-standing. The mechanism doesn’t care which way is up; the fasteners stay hidden in every direction.
