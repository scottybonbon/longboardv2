The line

Four stations, in the order the aluminum meets them. The material never leaves the building between them.

01 Extrusion Billet becomes profile. The presses are the reason “custom extrusion” is a phone call here and an import order elsewhere.
02 Vertical powder coating Profiles are coated hanging, in the same building that pressed them. The coat that the finish warranty stands on starts here.
03 Sublimation The grain image passes into the cured coat under heat and vacuum — the chemistry from the finish pages, at production scale.
04 AGV handling Automated guided vehicles carry the profiles between stations. The finish is handled by machines that don’t wear rings.
